## Design Context

### Users
This product serves Jordanian university students as the primary public audience, while also supporting contributors and admins who upload and manage academic materials. Users may arrive under time pressure, on mobile, and with a practical goal: find the right material quickly, understand what they need next, and stay academically organized without extra friction.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel helpful, credible, and youthful. The interface should prioritize clarity over decoration, communicate trust and usefulness immediately, and feel current without becoming trendy or noisy. The emotional goal is clarity first: users should feel oriented, reassured, and able to act quickly.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a minimal, utility-focused visual direction. Preserve the project's existing strengths: Arabic-first typography, clean card-based layouts, clear hierarchy, strong readability, and equally solid light and dark themes. Avoid overly detailed, crowded, or ornamental interfaces. The product should feel focused, modern, and efficient rather than expressive for its own sake.

### Design Principles
1. Optimize for speed to understanding: help users find materials, statuses, and next actions with minimal scanning.
2. Keep information organized and calm: reduce confusion through strong hierarchy, predictable layouts, and clear labels.
3. Default to functional minimalism: every visual element should serve comprehension or action, not decoration.
4. Preserve trust through consistency: maintain credible patterns, stable spacing, and accessible contrast across public and admin flows.
5. Design equally well for light and dark themes: neither mode should feel secondary or visually compromised.
